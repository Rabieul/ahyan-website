Our Story

A Decade of Building
Qatar's Future

Ahyan Trading & Contracting WLL was established in 2013 in Doha, Qatar, with a clear purpose — to deliver construction, engineering, and technical services with integrity, precision, and a genuine commitment to quality.

Over the past decade, we have grown from a focused contracting firm into a full-spectrum MEP and civil contractor, trusted by some of the most respected names in Qatar's construction industry.

Today, Ahyan holds ISO certifications in quality management, environmental responsibility, and occupational health and safety — a reflection of the standards we hold ourselves to on every project.
